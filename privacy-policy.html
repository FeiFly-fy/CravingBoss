Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Craving Boss ("the app", "we") is a game-based companion designed to help people manage smoking cravings through interactive battles and personal progress. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your journey stays on your device. Your smoking history, battle records, monster progress, and preferences are stored locally.
  • No account required. You can use Craving Boss without registering or providing personal contact information.
  • No tracking. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics and tracking SDKs.
  • You're in control. You can manage your records, reset your journey, export a backup, or choose to share a progress-card image.

Information stored on your device

Craving Boss stores your smoking history, daily cigarette consumption, battle history, journey progress, and app preferences locally on your device.

This information is used to personalize your experience and track your progress.

What we don't collect

Craving Boss does not request or collect your location information. The app also does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, home address, or contacts, and does not request access to your camera or microphone.

We do not use advertising identifiers, advertising SDKs, or third-party analytics and tracking services. Other than the information described in this policy, we do not collect additional personal information through the app.

Payments

All purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your card or payment details. Apple's privacy policy governs how it handles your payment information.

Backups and sharing

Craving Boss lets you manually create an encrypted backup of your journey. You choose where to save the backup file and are responsible for keeping its password secure.

You can also share a generated progress-card image through the iOS share sheet. Sharing the image does not transfer your underlying journey records. The receiving app or service may handle the image according to its own privacy practices.

Local app data is protected by available iOS security mechanisms, including app sandboxing. No storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. Notifications are optional and can be managed in the app or through iOS Settings.

Craving Boss does not use remote push notifications for these reminders.

Deleting your data

Your journey data is stored on your device. Deleting Craving Boss removes your locally saved journey records and progress from that device. We do not keep a separate copy of your journey data.

You can also use Reset My Journey to clear your journey without deleting the app.

Health information

Craving Boss is a motivational app designed to support your quit-smoking journey. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

For medical advice or professional help with quitting smoking, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Children's privacy

Craving Boss is intended for adults who want to manage smoking cravings. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy hosting

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use pages opened from Craving Boss may be hosted on third-party websites, including GitHub Pages. These pages are provided only to display legal documents and are not linked to your journey data.

Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app or its privacy practices. When we make changes, we will update the date shown at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated within the app.
