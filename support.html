Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases.

Make sure you're using the same Apple Account used for your original purchase.

This restores your paid access. Your journey records are stored locally on your device, and we do not keep copies of them. They cannot be recovered through Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us.

iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Craving Boss → Cancel Subscription.

On the web: Visit Apple Subscription Management, sign in with your Apple Account, and select Craving Boss to cancel your subscription.

Canceling your subscription normally lets you keep paid access until the end of the current billing period. Refunds are also handled by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

What happens if I buy Lifetime Access while subscribed monthly?

Lifetime Access is a separate one-time purchase.

Buying Lifetime Access does not automatically cancel your monthly subscription. To avoid future monthly charges, cancel your subscription through Apple.

Where is my data stored?

Your journey data is stored locally on your device. Craving Boss has no account system or server that stores your personal records.

Important: Both deleting the app and using Settings → Reset My Journey remove your locally saved journey records and progress. Please use either option carefully. Reinstalling the app will not automatically restore your previous progress.

Before resetting your journey, deleting the app, or changing devices, create an encrypted backup through Settings → Journey Backup. Keep your backup file and password safe.
